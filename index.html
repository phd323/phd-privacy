User Support & Legal Center

Step 1: Setup Your Contacts

The app requires a Contact profile to function. You have two options:

  • Manual: Person Icon (Bottom Right) → Contact → Add Contact.
  • Import: Person Icon → Contact → Import (Securely pull from your phone's local contacts).
Tip: Tap the (i) Info Icon in the entry screen to see which fields are required to enable the Save button.

Step 2: The "+New" Workflow

  1. Tap +New to open the action menu.
  2. Select an Action (Add, Edit, Report, or Manage) and a Profile (Moneyflow or Cosmetic).
  3. Select your Contact and tap Date →.

Step 3: Selection & Confirmation

  • Add: Pick a single date.
  • Edit/Manage/Report: Pick a date range.
  • The Selection Page: If your date range contains more than one result, you will be prompted to tap and select the specific entry you wish to view or modify.
  • Finalize: Tap the Save/New icon in the bottom sheet.

Step 4: System Feedback

All system alerts (success or error) are "Zero-Interaction":

  • No confirmation buttons (Yes/No/OK) are required.
  • Messages automatically dismiss after 3 seconds.
