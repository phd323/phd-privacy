Step 0: Subscription & Free Trial

On first launch, you will see the Paywall. Tap Continue to Purchase to begin your 14-day free trial — full access to all features from day one, managed by Apple. You can also enter a Promo Code if you have one, or tap Restore Previous Purchase if you have subscribed before.

Free Tier & Pro: Add and Manage are always free for Cosmetic and Moneyflow profiles. Edit, Report, Export/Import, and the Edu Profile require a PHD Pro subscription — which includes a 14-day free trial with full access from day one.

Step 1: Set Up Your Contacts

The app requires a Contact profile to function. Tap the Person Icon in the bottom right of the home page to open the menu:

  • Access Contacts: Tap the Contact Icon within that sub-menu to open your contact options (Add, Edit, Import, Manage).
  • Manual Entry: Tap Add Contact to enter details manually.
  • Import: Tap Import to securely pull existing contacts directly from your phone's local contact list.
Tip: In the Add or Edit screens, tap the (i) Info Icon in the bottom right to see which fields are required to enable the Save button.

Step 2: The "+New" Workflow

  1. Tap the +New button at the bottom of the home page.
  2. Select an Action (Add, Edit, Report, or Manage) and a Profile (Moneyflow, Cosmetic, or Edu).
  3. Select your Contact and tap the Date → button.

Step 3: Selection & Confirmation

  • Add Action: Pick a single date from the calendar.
  • Edit/Manage/Report: Pick a date range.
  • The Selection Page: If there is more than one result, you will be prompted to tap and select the specific entry in the selection page to proceed.

Step 4: System Feedback

  • Messages without a "Yes," "No," "OK," or "Confirm" button will be automatically dismissed after 3 seconds. Simply wait for the screen to clear.

Export / Import (Pro)

Tap the Person Icon and select Export to save a backup of your local data. Tap Import to restore from a previously exported file. Use this to transfer your data between devices anytime.

Restore & Reset

To restore a previous purchase or reset your data, visit the Admin Section in the app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

Our Core Commitment: Profile Hub Dossier is built on a local-first philosophy. Every single piece of profile data and personal record you create stays strictly on your device and is never uploaded, sold, or shared.

1. Who We Are

Profile Hub Dossier ("PHD", "we", "our") is an independent app development team. You can reach us at ProfileHubDossier@gmail.com for any privacy-related questions.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the absolute minimum necessary to operate the app:

  • Email Address: Your email is the key that links your account to your subscription and purchase history. It allows our team to verify your account status, restore access if needed, and provide support if you reach out. We do not use your email for marketing or spam.
  • Purchase & Subscription Status: Managed through RevenueCat to validate your subscription and restore purchases. No full payment or credit card details are ever stored by us.
  • Promo Code: If you use a promo code, we record it to apply your discount and to recognize the person who shared it with you.
Important: All profile content and personal records you enter into PHD are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see, access, or retrieve this data.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect your name, location, or phone number.
  • We do NOT use analytics SDKs to track your in-app behavior. Our subscription provider RevenueCat may collect limited product interaction data (such as paywall views) solely to manage your subscription — this data is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising.
  • We do NOT access your camera, microphone, or photo library without explicit permission.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your data to any third party, ever.

4. Third-Party Services

We use two trusted third-party services for core app functionality:

  • Google Firebase — Used to securely store your account credentials (email) for login and account recovery. Firebase operates under Google's privacy standards. View Firebase Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat — Used to manage subscriptions and restore purchases. RevenueCat securely receives your email and anonymized device ID to maintain your subscription status and allow our support team to locate your purchase history. View RevenueCat Privacy Policy →

No other third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics tools are used in this app.

5. Contact Permissions (Optional)

PHD can optionally import contacts from your phone's local address book to help you set up profiles faster. This is entirely optional — you can always add contacts manually. Contact data pulled into the app remains on your device and is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with any server or third party under any circumstance.

6. Data Security

We take security seriously:

  • Account credentials are stored in Google Firebase with industry-standard encryption.
  • All profile and personal data lives on your local device, protected by your device's built-in security (Face ID, passcode, etc.).
  • We recommend keeping your device and the app updated to benefit from the latest security improvements.

7. Your Rights

You are in full control of your data:

  • Delete your account: Contact us at ProfileHubDossier@gmail.com and we will permanently delete your account data (email, UID, and promo code if applicable) from our records.
  • Delete your local data: Use the in-app Reset button to wipe all local data instantly, or simply uninstall the app. Either way, all profile data is permanently removed from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

PHD is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the app grows and adds new features. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Profile Hub Dossier reserves the right of final interpretation regarding the terms and explanations within this policy. Your continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your formal acceptance of the updated policy.
