Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profile Hub Dossier (PHD) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our app. We only collect what is necessary to provide you with a working, secure experience.

By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Application (App): Profile Hub Dossier (PHD).
Personal Data: Information that can identify you — specifically your email address and purchase history.
We / Us / Our: The developer of Profile Hub Dossier.

2. What We Collect and Why

Personal Data

We may ask for the following information:

  • Email Address: Used to identify your account, restore your purchase if you reinstall the app, and contact you for support. Providing your email is optional but recommended to protect your purchase.

Usage Data

We automatically collect limited data to keep the app running smoothly:

  • App Statistics: Number of app opens, last open date, and current app version.
  • Device Platform: Whether you are on iOS or Android.
  • Device Identifier (IDFV): A vendor-specific device identifier provided by iOS. Used solely to prevent trial abuse (e.g. detecting reinstalls). This is not an advertising identifier and cannot be used to track you across other apps.

We do not collect your profile content. All data you enter into your Core Profiles stays on your device only.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

To manage your access to premium features, we work with trusted third-party services:

Note: Your email is used to link your account records with RevenueCat. This allows us to restore your purchase history if you reinstall the app or switch devices.

  • Apple App Store: Handles all payments. We never see or store your credit card details.
  • RevenueCat: Tracks your subscription status, trial period, renewals, and cancellations.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To keep your account active and your subscription status up to date.
  • To restore your purchase if you reinstall the app.
  • To contact you about important account or service updates.
  • To monitor app performance and fix issues.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Your account data — including your email, subscription dates, and app usage stats — is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), which encrypts data both at rest and in transit.

We keep your data only as long as needed. If you delete your account, all personal data is removed within 30 days. Anonymized purchase records may be retained longer for legal and accounting purposes as required by Apple.

6. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your data. We only share data with the following services that are essential to running the app:

  • Google LLC (Firebase): Data storage and anonymous authentication.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Subscription and purchase management.
  • Apple Inc.: Payment processing.

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can delete all your personal data at any time directly inside the app under Settings → Delete My Data. This removes everything we hold about you from our servers. To cancel an active subscription, visit your App Store subscription settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
