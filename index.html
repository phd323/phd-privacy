Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the Profile Hub Dossier (PHD) application and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

We use Your Personal data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Account: A unique account created for You to access our Service or parts of our Service.
Application (App): Refers to Profile Hub Dossier (PHD), the software program provided by the Company.
Personal Data: Any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual (specifically Email and Purchase History).

2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data: While using Our Application, We may ask You to provide Us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify You. This includes:

  • Email Address: This is used as the primary identifier to maintain your account, synchronize data, and serve as a lookup key for purchase records and subscription status.
  • Profile Content: Data manually entered into the "Core Profiles" (financial, cosmetic, or contact records).

Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information to ensure app stability and performance:

  • App Statistics: Total App Opens, Last Open Date, and App Version.
  • Device Info: Operating system platform (iOS or Android).

3. Purchase and Subscription Tracking

To provide premium features and maintain your "Core" or "Subscription" status, we use third-party services for payment processing and entitlement management.

Important: Your Email Address is used to cross-check records between our database and RevenueCat. This ensures that if you change devices, your active subscription and purchase history are accurately restored.

  • Apple App Store: All financial transactions are processed by Apple. We do not store credit card numbers.
  • RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to track subscription lifecycle events (trial start, renewals, cancellations). RevenueCat acts as our source of truth for your purchase status.

4. Use of Your Personal Data

The Company may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your Service: Including monitoring the usage of our Service.
  • To manage Your Account: Your email identifies you as a user and allows access to your specific Core Profiles.
  • To manage Purchases: Using your email and purchase tokens to validate access to premium tiers.
  • To contact You: To provide technical support or critical updates regarding your account.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Google Firebase: Your data is stored securely using Firebase (Firestore). Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. This includes your Core Profile Data and account metadata.

Retention: We will retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. If you request account deletion, we will delete all profile data within 30 days, though anonymized purchase logs may be kept for legal and accounting compliance as required by Apple.

6. Disclosure of Your Personal Data

We do not sell your data. We only share data with the following essential service providers:

  • Google LLC (Firebase): For data hosting and authentication.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: For subscription management and entitlement tracking.
  • Apple Inc.: For payment processing.

7. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data that We have collected about You. You may delete your account and all associated data directly within the App settings, or by contacting us at the email address provided below.
