Step 1: Setup Your Contacts

The app requires a Contact profile to function. Tap the Person Icon in the bottom right of the home page to open the menu:

  • Access Contacts: Tap the Contact Icon within that sub-menu to open your contact options (Add, Edit, Import, Manage).
  • Manual Entry: Tap Add Contact to enter details manually.
  • Import: Tap Import to securely pull existing contacts directly from your phone's local contact list.
Tip: In the Add or Edit screens, tap the (i) Info Icon in the bottom right to see which fields are required to enable the Save button.

Step 2: The "+New" Workflow

  1. Tap the +New button at the bottom of the home page.
  2. Select an Action (Add, Edit, Report, or Manage) and a Profile (Moneyflow or Cosmetic).
  3. Select your Contact and tap the Date → button.

Step 3: Selection & Confirmation

  • Add Action: Pick a single date from the calendar.
  • Edit/Manage/Report: Pick a date range.
  • The Selection Page: In some cases, if there are more than one result, you will be prompted to tap and select the specific entry in the selection page to proceed.

Step 4: System Feedback

  • Messages without a "Yes," "No," "OK," or "Confirm" button will be automatically dismissed after 3 seconds. Simply wait for the screen to clear.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

Profile Hub Dossier (PHD) is committed to your privacy. We only collect essential information required to manage your account and restore purchases.

Data Ownership: All profile content, inventory details, and financial logs stay strictly on your local device. We do not collect or see your personal dossiers.

1. Data Collection

  • Email: Used for account recovery and identifying subscription status.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous statistics (app opens, version) to help us improve performance.
  • IDFV: A vendor-specific identifier used solely to prevent trial abuse on iOS.

2. Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase for secure data storage and RevenueCat for subscription management. Payments are handled exclusively by the Apple App Store.
