Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Profile Hub Dossier (PHD) collects, uses, and protects your information. We prioritize your privacy and only collect what is essential for secure app functionality.

1. Interpretation and Definitions

Application (App): Profile Hub Dossier (PHD).
Personal Data: Information that can identify you — specifically your email address and purchase history.
We / Us / Our: The developer of Profile Hub Dossier.

2. What We Collect and Why

Personal Data

  • Email Address: Used to identify your account and restore purchases.

Usage Data

  • App Statistics: Number of app opens and version tracking.
  • Device Identifier (IDFV): Used solely to prevent trial abuse on iOS. Not used for tracking across other apps.

Core Privacy: We do NOT collect your profile content. All data you enter into your Core Profiles stays on your device only.

3. Purchases and Subscriptions

When you purchase through Sign In with Apple, your Apple ID identifier is used by RevenueCat to manage your subscription status securely.

4. Data Storage

Account data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore), encrypted at rest and in transit.

Questions? Reach us at: ProfileHubDossier@gmail.com
